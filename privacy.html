Privacy Policy

EDU Quiz Lanka · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Last updated: 20 August 2026
Developer: EDU Labs™ (operated by Shanaka Wijesinghe) · Contact: privacy@eduquizlk.com

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data EDU Quiz Lanka ("the App") collects, why we collect it, the legal bases we rely on, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have — including the additional rights of users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Data controller: EDU Labs™, operated by Shanaka Wijesinghe. Contact: privacy@eduquizlk.com.

1. Personal Data We Collect

  • Account & profile — first and family name, email address, date of birth, grade, district, and optionally your school name, phone number, avatar, and frame. Your password is stored only as a secure hash. Date of birth is required: we use it to apply age-appropriate ad settings and child-safety protections.
  • Country — to show your country flag on leaderboards we detect your country (only country-level, never your precise position). If you have granted location permission we read one coarse, lowest-accuracy location fix; otherwise we derive the country from your IP address on our server. You can deny location permission and the App still works.
  • Device information — a device identifier used to enforce the free-games allowance and for anti-cheat, plus device model/OS data included in crash reports.
  • Gameplay data — quiz scores, game history, win/loss records, subject performance, season totals, tournament entries, and Hall of Fame rankings.
  • Purchases & virtual currency — pass and premium purchases (processed by the Apple App Store / Google Play; we never see your card details), and your coin/gem balances and transaction history.
  • Push notifications — a push token, if notifications are enabled on your device.
  • Prize delivery (physical-prize tournament winners only) — full name, delivery address, phone number, and national identity card (NIC) number; for winners under 18 we instead collect a parent/guardian's name, relationship, NIC, and phone, with the guardian's consent. This data is used only to deliver the prize and satisfy legal record-keeping.
  • Usage & crash data — crash logs and basic usage statistics via Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics. In the EEA/UK these are OFF by default and only activated if you consent (see Section 4).

2. Why We Process Your Data (Legal Bases)

  • Performance of our contract with you — creating and running your account, gameplay, leaderboards, purchases, prize delivery, support.
  • Your consent — personalised advertising, analytics and crash reporting in the EEA/UK, and optional location permission. You can withdraw consent at any time (Section 4).
  • Legitimate interests — fair play and anti-cheat (device checks, score validation), service security, serving NON-personalised ads to keep the App free, and defending legal claims.
  • Legal obligation — tax, accounting, and prize-competition record-keeping.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Advertising

The App is free and supported by advertising served through Google AdMob.

Users under 18 only ever receive non-personalised, age-rated ads. Users we know or believe to be under 13 receive child-directed treatment (Google's strictest setting).

In the EEA/UK, on first launch you will see a consent form (Google's certified consent platform). Personalised ads are shown only if you are an adult AND you consented in that form. If you decline, you receive non-personalised ads or, where consent for any ads is refused, no ads (some free-play features that are unlocked by watching an ad may then be unavailable).

On iOS we additionally use Apple's App Tracking Transparency: without your permission there, ads are non-personalised regardless of other settings.

You can change or withdraw your ad-consent choices at any time in Settings → "Privacy & ad consent". Battle Pass holders do not see standard ads.

4. Analytics, Crash Reporting & Your Consent Controls

We use Firebase Analytics (usage statistics) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) to fix bugs and improve the App.

In the EEA/UK both are DISABLED by default and are only switched on if you give consent in the consent form. If you refuse or later withdraw consent, they stay off.

Withdrawing consent: Settings → "Privacy & ad consent" reopens the consent options at any time. iOS tracking permission can be changed in the device Settings app.

5. Who We Share Data With (Processors)

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and server functions (hosted in Singapore)
  • Google Firebase — push notifications, crash reporting, analytics, remote configuration
  • Google AdMob — advertising (with the consent controls described above)
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase and subscription management
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing

Physical prize delivery may additionally require sharing your delivery details with a courier service.

6. International Transfers

Our primary database is hosted by Supabase in Singapore; Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States and other countries. Where data of EEA/UK users is transferred outside the EEA/UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses as implemented in our processors' data-processing agreements.

7. How Long We Keep Data (Retention)

  • Account and gameplay data — for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account) your account row and all dependent personal data are deleted immediately from the live database; residual copies in encrypted backups expire within 30 days.
  • Prize-delivery data (including NIC numbers) — automatically erased 90 days after the prize is delivered, or earlier on request where no legal obligation requires keeping it.
  • Purchase records — kept as required by tax and accounting law.
  • Crash and analytics data — retained according to Firebase's standard retention periods.

8. Children's Privacy

EDU Quiz Lanka is an educational app used by students aged approximately 9 and above. We take children's privacy seriously:

  • Users under 18 never receive personalised ads; under-13s receive child-directed ad treatment.
  • In the EEA/UK, analytics stay off for everyone unless valid consent is given.
  • Physical prizes for winners under 18 are delivered to a parent or guardian, with the guardian's details collected only with their consent.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data that should be removed, contact privacy@eduquizlk.com and we will delete it promptly. You can also use Settings → Delete account on the child's device at any time.

9. Security

All data travels over HTTPS/TLS and is encrypted at rest. Access to production data is restricted. Sensitive tables (such as prize-delivery details) are locked so that they can only be reached through audited server functions. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data we will notify you and the competent supervisory authority as required by law.

10. Your Rights

You can exercise these rights directly in the App or by emailing privacy@eduquizlk.com:

  • Access & portability — Settings → "Export my data" gives you a machine-readable JSON file of all data linked to your account.
  • Rectification — correct your profile details in the App, or ask us.
  • Erasure — Settings → "Delete account" permanently removes your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent — Settings → "Privacy & ad consent" (ads/analytics), device settings (iOS tracking, location).
  • Objection & restriction — you may object to processing based on legitimate interests, or ask us to restrict processing, by emailing us.
  • Complaint — EEA/UK users may lodge a complaint with their national data-protection authority (in France, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr).

We respond to rights requests within one month, as required by the GDPR.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and notify you via an in-app notice if the changes are material.
