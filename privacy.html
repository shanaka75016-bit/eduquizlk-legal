Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data EDU Quiz Prime ("the App") collects, why we collect it, the legal bases we rely on, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have — including the additional rights of users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Data controller: MERU LAB, operated by Shanaka Wijesinghe. Contact: edulabslanka@gmail.com.

1. Personal Data We Collect

  • Optional social sign-in — if you choose Google, Facebook, or Apple on a supported device, Supabase receives a provider account identifier and the information shared for sign-in, such as your email address and any basic profile details the provider returns. Apple may supply a private relay email address. We use this information to authenticate your account; public gameplay uses your chosen username. We do not receive your Google, Facebook, or Apple password.
  • Account & profile — new registration asks for email, username, a broad age range (under 13, 13–15, 16–17, or 18 or older), and district or overseas status. We do not ask new players for a birth date, real name, or phone number. Players declaring an age below 18 and a Sri Lankan district may optionally choose a government school from that district or the generic Private school option, then a supported grade. The 18-or-older and overseas registration flows do not show the school picker. For Sri Lankan profiles, province is derived from the selected district. Your password is stored only as a secure hash. The age range is used for age-appropriate ads and safety controls.
  • Optional school participation — if you choose government-school rankings, we store your selected school, grade, participation status, a generated school-only player alias, and verified Academic Arena point contributions. Your school affiliation is kept in private records and is not shown on your public profile. The internal grade board is available only to participating accounts in the same government school and displays generated aliases instead of usernames, names, profile links, or account IDs. School standings show combined points only when at least five participating players have contributed to the selected season and grade scope; contributor counts are rounded down to groups of five.
  • School selection — named school standings support government national and provincial schools. Private-school players can register and use individual Academic Arena grade rankings; we record a generic private-school marker and grade without collecting the private institution name. This choice creates no school-directory entry or displayed school alias and earns no School Points. School and age details are declarations by the player; we do not independently verify school attendance. If email confirmation is needed before setup can finish, an optional school-selection draft is kept in encrypted storage on that device for the account until setup is completed or the selection is cleared. School affiliation is not placed in public authentication metadata.
  • Older account details — some older accounts still have a date of birth or other profile details provided under an earlier registration flow. We retain those legacy details under this policy. Public rank boards use usernames by default; an eligible older account may opt in to showing its real name.
  • Ad-hoc detail requests — occasionally we may ask you, via your in-app inbox, for specific details for a stated reason (for example verifying a prize delivery). Such requests show exactly which details are requested and why; providing them is optional (you can decline), requires your explicit consent, and the data is deleted no later than 90 days after the request closes.
  • Country — to show your country flag on leaderboards we detect your country (only country-level, never your precise position). If you have granted location permission we read one coarse, lowest-accuracy location fix; otherwise we derive the country from your IP address on our server. You can deny location permission and the App still works.
  • Device information — a device identifier used to enforce the free-games allowance and for anti-cheat, plus device model/OS data included in crash reports.
  • Gameplay data — quiz scores, game history, win/loss records, subject performance, season totals, tournament entries, and Hall of Fame rankings.
  • Purchases & virtual currency — pass and premium purchases (processed by the Apple App Store / Google Play; we never see your card details), and your coin/gem balances and transaction history.
  • Push notifications — a push token, if notifications are enabled on your device.
  • Prize delivery (physical-prize tournament winners only) — full name, delivery address, phone number, and national identity card (NIC) number; for winners under 18 we instead collect a parent/guardian's name, relationship, NIC, and phone, with the guardian's consent. This data is used only to deliver the prize and satisfy legal record-keeping.
  • Usage & crash data — crash logs and basic usage statistics via Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics. In the EEA/UK these are OFF by default and only activated if you consent (see Section 4).

2. Why We Process Your Data (Legal Bases)

  • Performance of our contract with you — creating and running your account, gameplay, leaderboards, purchases, prize delivery, support.
  • Your consent — personalised advertising, analytics and crash reporting in the EEA/UK, and optional location permission. You can withdraw consent at any time (Section 4).
  • Legitimate interests — fair play and anti-cheat (device checks, score validation), service security, serving NON-personalised ads to keep the App free, and defending legal claims.
  • Legal obligation — tax, accounting, and prize-competition record-keeping.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Advertising

The App is free and supported by advertising served through Google AdMob.

Users who declare an age below 18 receive non-personalised, age-rated ads. Under-13 and unknown-age accounts receive child-directed treatment. The current ad SDK also applies child-level ad restrictions to teen accounts until a distinct teen treatment is supported.

In the EEA/UK, adults may see a consent form (Google's certified consent platform). We do not request ad consent from children or teens. Personalised ads are shown only if you are an adult AND you consented in that form. If you decline, you receive non-personalised ads or, where consent for any ads is refused, no ads (some free-play features that are unlocked by watching an ad may then be unavailable).

On iOS we additionally use Apple's App Tracking Transparency: without your permission there, ads are non-personalised regardless of other settings.

You can change or withdraw your ad-consent choices at any time in Settings → "Privacy & ad consent". Battle Pass holders do not see standard ads.

4. Analytics, Crash Reporting & Your Consent Controls

We use Firebase Analytics (usage statistics) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) to fix bugs and improve the App.

Analytics and crash collection are disabled for child, teen, and unknown-age accounts. For adults in the EEA/UK they remain off unless consent is given; withdrawing consent switches them off again.

Withdrawing consent: Settings → "Privacy & ad consent" reopens the consent options at any time. iOS tracking permission can be changed in the device Settings app.

5. Who We Share Data With (Processors)

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and server functions (hosted in Singapore)
  • Google Firebase — push notifications, crash reporting, analytics, remote configuration
  • Google AdMob — advertising (with the consent controls described above)
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase and subscription management
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing

Optional sign-in providers — Google, Meta (Facebook), or Apple process your chosen sign-in under their own privacy policies and learn that you are signing in to EDU Quiz Prime. Only the provider you choose is used for that sign-in.

Physical prize delivery may additionally require sharing your delivery details with a courier service.

6. International Transfers

Our primary database is hosted by Supabase in Singapore; Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States and other countries. Where data of EEA/UK users is transferred outside the EEA/UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses as implemented in our processors' data-processing agreements.

7. How Long We Keep Data (Retention)

  • Social sign-in access — deleting your EDU Quiz Prime account does not delete your Google, Facebook, or Apple account. For Apple-linked accounts, the App attempts to revoke Apple access during deletion on supported iOS devices. If automatic revocation is unavailable, account deletion still proceeds and the App explains how to remove access in your Apple Account settings. You can also remove this app from your sign-in provider account settings.
  • Account and gameplay data — for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account) your account row and all dependent personal data are deleted immediately from the live database; residual copies in encrypted backups expire within 30 days.
  • School participation — you can leave through the school participation screen. Leaving stops new school contributions and removes your school alias and contributions from displayed school boards. Private membership and verified point records remain while your account exists for fair-play checks and ranking history; deleting your account removes the dependent school records.
  • Prize-delivery data (including NIC numbers) — automatically erased 90 days after the prize is delivered, or earlier on request where no legal obligation requires keeping it.
  • Purchase records — kept as required by tax and accounting law.
  • Crash and analytics data — retained according to Firebase's standard retention periods.

8. Children's Privacy

EDU Quiz Prime is an educational app used by students aged approximately 9 and above. We take children's privacy seriously:

  • Users under 18 never receive personalised ads; under-13s receive child-directed ad treatment.
  • In the EEA/UK, analytics stay off for everyone unless valid consent is given.
  • Physical prizes for winners under 18 are delivered to a parent or guardian, with the guardian's details collected only with their consent.
  • Optional school participation does not publish a child's school affiliation on their public profile. Internal school boards use generated aliases and school standings require at least five contributors before totals are displayed.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data that should be removed, contact edulabslanka@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly. You can also use Settings → Delete account on the child's device at any time.

9. Security

All data travels over HTTPS/TLS and is encrypted at rest. Access to production data is restricted. Sensitive tables (such as prize-delivery details) are locked so that they can only be reached through audited server functions. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data we will notify you and the competent supervisory authority as required by law.

10. Your Rights

You can exercise these rights directly in the App or by emailing edulabslanka@gmail.com:

  • Access & portability — email edulabslanka@gmail.com from your account email and we will send you a machine-readable copy of all data linked to your account within 30 days.
  • Rectification — correct your profile details in the App, or ask us.
  • Erasure — Settings → "Delete account" permanently removes your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent — Settings → "Privacy & ad consent" (ads/analytics), device settings (iOS tracking, location).
  • Objection & restriction — you may object to processing based on legitimate interests, or ask us to restrict processing, by emailing us.
  • Complaint — EEA/UK users may lodge a complaint with their national data-protection authority (in France, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr).

We respond to rights requests within one month, as required by the GDPR.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and notify you via an in-app notice if the changes are material.
